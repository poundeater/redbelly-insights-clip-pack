Method

How the pack was made

Every step is a script in tools/, driven by clips.json, so the whole pack can be rebuilt and checked from the official episodes.

01 SOURCES

Official episodes only

The episode files linked from redbelly.network/ama. Episodes 26 to 29 were transcribed in full. No music, stock footage or third party material is added.

02 SELECTION

One passage, one point

Each clip is a single uninterrupted passage about Redbelly technology, Project Acacia or Tokeniser. No internal cuts, so no quote is joined to words said elsewhere. No two clips make the same point.

03 FRAMING AND CAPTIONS

Speaker in shot, words checked

Faces are tracked every 0.2 s to set a 9:16 crop per camera shot. Caption text is proof-read against three separate Whisper transcripts, and names and terms are fixed by hand.

04 AUDIO AND CHECKS

Even loudness, measured output

Two-pass EBU R128 normalisation to -14 LUFS. The verify step measures specs, length, caption coverage, spelling, framing, loudness and a personal-credit scan for every file.

export WORK=$PWD/work
tools/fetch.sh                                   # official episodes, audio, face model
python3 tools/transcribe.py small.en $WORK/aud/ep26.wav $WORK/tx/ep26.json
python3 tools/transcribe.py small.en $WORK/aud/ep28.wav $WORK/tx/ep28.json
python3 tools/build.py                           # clips/*.mp4 and subtitles/*.srt
python3 tools/verify.py                          # verification/REPORT.md and report.json
python3 tools/clip_list.py                       # CLIP_LIST.md
python3 tools/site.py                            # this page, posters and og.png
