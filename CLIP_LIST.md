## Details

### 1. Sandwich attacks on Redbelly get detected. Then punished.

- **File:** `clips/01_mev_attacks_get_caught.mp4`. Subtitles are burned in and also in `subtitles/01_mev_attacks_get_caught.srt`.
- **Source:** Redbelly Insights - Episode 28, 11 June 2026. Video: https://storage.googleapis.com/redbelly-website.firebasestorage.app/Video/rb_ep_28_final_745719acdb/rb_ep_28_final_745719acdb.mp4
- **Timestamp:** 16:05.40 to 16:58.00
- **Speaker:** Vincent Gramoli, Founder & Chief Science Officer, Redbelly Network
- **Topic:** Redbelly technology: MEV accountability
- **The one point:** Redbelly detects maximum extractable value (MEV) attacks such as sandwich and front-running attacks, and can automatically penalise repeat offenders by slashing stake or removing them for a while.
- **Suggested post caption:** Front-running and sandwich attacks drain ordinary users on most chains. On Redbelly they are detected, and repeat offenders can lose stake.

### 2. No forks. No double spending. Why that matters for big assets.

- **File:** `clips/02_no_forks_no_double_spend.mp4`. Subtitles are burned in and also in `subtitles/02_no_forks_no_double_spend.srt`.
- **Source:** Redbelly Insights - Episode 28, 11 June 2026. Video: https://storage.googleapis.com/redbelly-website.firebasestorage.app/Video/rb_ep_28_final_745719acdb/rb_ep_28_final_745719acdb.mp4
- **Timestamp:** 0:53.35 to 1:29.60
- **Speaker:** Vincent Gramoli, Founder & Chief Science Officer, Redbelly Network
- **Topic:** Redbelly technology: certainty for high-value assets
- **The one point:** Redbelly is seen as an alternative layer one for high-value asset applications because it gives certainty: no forks and no double-spending risk.
- **Suggested post caption:** If you are putting high-value assets on a blockchain, forks and double spends are not acceptable. Vincent Gramoli on why that is where Redbelly fits.
- **Note:** The host's line (shown in italics, marked HOST in the .srt) is kept because it names the chains Vincent is comparing against.

### 3. “Public digital token infrastructure”, in 45 seconds

- **File:** `clips/03_what_pdti_means.mp4`. Subtitles are burned in and also in `subtitles/03_what_pdti_means.srt`.
- **Source:** Redbelly Insights - Episode 28, 11 June 2026. Video: https://storage.googleapis.com/redbelly-website.firebasestorage.app/Video/rb_ep_28_final_745719acdb/rb_ep_28_final_745719acdb.mp4

- **Speaker:** Vincent Gramoli, Founder & Chief Science Officer, Redbelly Network
- **Topic:** Redbelly technology: public digital token infrastructure
- **The one point:** As public digital token infrastructure (PDTI), Redbelly is permissionless, open-source, compliant with the law and secure enough to protect users, and is a platform that connects people without needing an AFSL.
- **Suggested post caption:** Permissionless, open source, compliant with the law: Vincent Gramoli on what it means for Redbelly to be public digital token infrastructure (PDTI).

### 4. Sell your fund units without waiting for the fund

- **File:** `clips/04_acacia_investors_trade_directly.mp4`. Subtitles are burned in and also in `subtitles/04_acacia_investors_trade_directly.srt`.
- **Source:** Redbelly Insights - Episode 26, 26 March 2026. Video: https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/redbelly-website.firebasestorage.app/o/RBI_EP26.mp4?alt=media&token=ce10a8dd-1721-4272-bd43-f3e6be081d94
- **Timestamp:** 21:22.25 to 22:07.05
- **Speaker:** Matt Mills, Chief Customer Officer, Tokeniser
- **Topic:** Project Acacia + Tokeniser: holder-to-holder transfers
- **The one point:** In Project Acacia, unit holders could agree a price and swap units directly with each other (the fund only approves the transfer), and anyone can verify it on the Redbelly blockchain instead of trusting Tokeniser.
- **Suggested post caption:** In Project Acacia, fund investors could sell units straight to another holder at an agreed price, and anyone could check the trade on the Redbelly blockchain.
